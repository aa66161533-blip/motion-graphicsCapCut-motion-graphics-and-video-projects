# Asset provenance

## Tiger anatomical study

- Author: Innerscene Corp
- Source: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/tiger-3a01b408
- Download: https://www.innerscene.com/api/library/tiger-3a01b408/download
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- The source page and the GLB's embedded `asset.copyright` explicitly dedicate the model to the public domain, allowing use, modification and redistribution, including commercial use.
- Local asset: `tiger_cc0.glb`.
- Adaptations: authored 28-bone rig, inverse-kinematics walking, eyelid shape keys, a cut lip boundary, mouth cavity, tongue and canines, head/jaw/tail animation, and ink-to-volume transformation.
- The model was not originally animated. The motion in this project is authored and baked in Blender.

## Hanji landscape

- Original image generated with OpenAI's image-generation tool for this project.
- Local asset: `hanji_landscape.png`.
- Contains mountains, pines, rocks and paper only. No tiger, lettering, seal or watermark is embedded in the landscape image.

The final film contains no on-screen credit text, logo or watermark. The CC0 tiger licence does not require on-screen attribution.
